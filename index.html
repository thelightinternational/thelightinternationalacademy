About The Light International Academy

The Light International Academy is a Cambridge-based school dedicated to helping students develop academically, personally, and confidently.

We believe every student learns differently. Our approach focuses on individual growth, strong academic foundations, and creating an environment where students can discover their strengths and reach their potential.

Our Academic Programs

Students progress through a structured Cambridge-based education pathway from lower secondary through A Levels.

Lower Secondary

Grades 7–9

Building strong academic foundations while helping students develop confidence, independence, and effective learning habits.

IGCSE

Grades 10–11

Cambridge IGCSE education designed to develop knowledge, critical thinking, problem-solving, and preparation for advanced study.

A Levels

AS Year 12 & A2 Year 13

Advanced Cambridge qualifications preparing students for university and their chosen future pathways.
